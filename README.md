# Smart Medical Equipment Lifecycle Management System

## Overview

Smart Medical Equipment Lifecycle Management System is an AI-powered platform designed to help hospitals efficiently manage medical devices throughout their entire lifecycle.

The system enables healthcare organizations to monitor equipment status, track maintenance activities, predict failures before they occur, and optimize resource utilization.

## Problem

Hospitals often face:

* Unexpected medical equipment failures
* High maintenance costs
* Lack of centralized equipment records
* Delayed repair processes
* Poor visibility into equipment performance

These challenges can affect healthcare quality and operational efficiency.

## Solution

Our platform provides a centralized and intelligent system that:

* Tracks all medical equipment assets
* Automates maintenance scheduling
* Predicts equipment failures using AI
* Manages spare parts inventory
* Generates maintenance reports and analytics
* Supports QR code-based equipment identification
